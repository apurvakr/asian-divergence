Reading the data

Source. Maddison Project Database 2023 (Bolt and van Zanden), via Our World in Data. Real GDP per capita in 2011 international dollars, so levels compare across countries and across time. Values are rounded to the dollar.

The 1940s. Annual series only begin in 1950. Earlier points are benchmarks: Korea and Taiwan were Japanese colonies, so their pre-1945 figures are colonial-era reconstructions that do not map cleanly onto today's states; China has a single pre-war point; Hong Kong has none. Treat the dashed segments as orders of magnitude.

China. Maddison's China series runs higher in later decades than some alternative reconstructions, and its crossover with India comes earlier than nominal-dollar comparisons suggest. The direction of the story does not change; the dates are softer than they look.

Selection. Japan and the four tigers are the region's winners. Comparing India only with them overstates how unusual India's path was; Indonesia, the Philippines or Thailand would make a less flattering and more honest control group.
